# Research Findings

Last reviewed: 2026-08-07.

Use this folder for concise, source-grounded summaries that can support ADRs, plans, and benchmark gates.

## Finding Template

```md
# Finding: Short Title

Status: Draft
Research area:
Last reviewed:

## Sources

- Source title, URL or local metadata file, accessed date.

## Claim

State the specific technical claim.

## Evidence

Summarize the evidence in your own words. Keep direct quotes short.

## Applicability To Gethen

Explain how strongly this applies to Gethen's alpha scope and constraints.

## Confidence

Low, medium, or high, with a short reason.

## Decision Impact

Supports, weakens, blocks, or requires a benchmark for a proposed decision.

## Follow-Up

List the next prototype, benchmark, accessibility test, or license check.
```
